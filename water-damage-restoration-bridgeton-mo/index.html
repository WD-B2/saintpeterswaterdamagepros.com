Overview

Bridgeton at a glance for water damage calls

Bridgeton is a city in northwestern St. Louis County. The 2020 census counted 11,445 residents. The populated areas sit between Lambert airport and St. Charles County, and portions of the airport are within the city. If water is spreading through a home or business here, call the 24/7 line at (636) 747-8964.

Bridgeton was incorporated in 1843, and its housing spans many decades. Supply lines, drain lines and roofs of different eras behave differently, and the first moisture reading shows what you are dealing with.

Local Facts

Bridgeton facts that matter

  • The Missouri River is the northwestern boundary of the city, which is why you should check the FEMA flood map for your address.
  • The city is served by the Pattonville School District and has a St. Louis County Library branch at Bridgeton Trails.
  • Interstate 70 and the outer belt meet in Bridgeton, so commercial and distribution properties are common.
  • ZIP 63044 is the main Bridgeton ZIP, and the part of Bridgeton that uses 63045 shares it with Earth City.
  • The city code sets up a Sewer Lateral Repair Fund, paid by a sewer lateral fee, for defective residential lateral lines.
  • The city code reimburses approved repairs up to a set amount through the contractor, so confirm the current amount with the city.
First Steps

If Water Is Still Spreading

STEP 1

Stop the water

Close the fixture or main valve. If the water is coming up a drain, stop using every sink, toilet and washer.

STEP 2

Stay out of contaminated water

Water from a drain or outside is Category 3. Keep people and pets out and do not wade in.

STEP 3

Control power

Switch off power to the affected area only from dry ground, or call the utility.

STEP 4

Document the loss

Photograph the water and damaged items, and contact your insurer.

Sewer Lines

Sewer lines and the city fund

Sewer backups are one of the more common calls in older St. Louis County suburbs, and Bridgeton has a program aimed at the lateral line between the house and the main. The program pays for the line repair and not for cleanup inside the house. A plumber clears or repairs the line, and the restoration crew handles the water, as described on the sewage backup cleanup page.

Keep records of the plumber's findings and the cleanup. Your insurer, the city and your contractor may each want a different piece of paper.

Common Questions

Bridgeton water damage questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Bridgeton?

Yes. We serve addresses in 63044 and the part of Bridgeton that uses 63045, which is shared with Earth City. Give the dispatcher your street address and it is confirmed on the call.

Is emergency water removal available in Bridgeton?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at any hour, and crews are sent as soon as one is available. The dispatcher helps you shut off water and protect belongings while you wait.

Does Bridgeton help pay for sewer line repairs?

The Bridgeton city code establishes a Sewer Lateral Repair Fund paid for by a sewer lateral fee, and it reimburses approved residential repairs under its own limits. Rules and amounts can change, so ask the City of Bridgeton before you hire a contractor.

Is a sewer backup in Bridgeton covered by homeowners insurance?

Many policies exclude sewer backup unless a water backup endorsement was added. Check your policy and ask your agent. The city fund covers a defective lateral line and not the cleanup inside the house.

Can you handle water damage in a Bridgeton business?

Yes. Bridgeton has many commercial properties, and the commercial water damage page explains how those jobs run, including landlord and carrier notification.
