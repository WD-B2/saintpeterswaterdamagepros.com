Structural Drying and Dehumidification in Saint Peters, MO

Water that is out of sight is still in the structure. Air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and daily moisture readings dry framing, subfloor and wall cavities to a measurable standard.

Symptoms

Is your house still wet after a leak?

Many people searching for structural drying do not use the phrase. They write that the wall still feels damp, the floor smells musty, the basement never fully dried or the paint is bubbling. Those are the signs of moisture left in the structure after the visible water is gone.

A musty smell

Persistent smell after cleanup usually means moisture remains in drywall, insulation or subfloor.

Swollen baseboards or trim

Wood and MDF absorb moisture and swell at the wall base.

Cool, damp-feeling drywall

Moisture in a wall lowers its surface temperature and softens the paper.

High humidity indoors

A house that feels sticky after a leak is releasing moisture from wet materials.

If you see those signs, a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera show what is wet. Ask also about mold if the smell has lasted more than a day or two.

The Method

How structural drying works

Drying follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 approach. Water leaves materials as vapor, and that vapor has to be removed from the air or it settles back into the structure. Air movers speed evaporation from surfaces, and LGR dehumidifiers pull the vapor out of the room so the air stays dry enough to keep evaporating.

The crew places equipment according to the wet areas, not by a fixed formula. Wall cavities may be opened with small holes or by removing baseboards. Floor cavities can be dried with mats and ports. Moisture readings are taken each visit, and the equipment is adjusted until the structure reaches the dry standard set for that material.

Equipment and its job
EquipmentWhat it does
Air moversPush air over wet surfaces and into cavities to speed evaporation.

Decision

Drying versus replacing

Not everything that got wet needs to be removed. Clean-water drywall that is caught early may dry in place, and solid framing almost always dries. Insulation, soft drywall and padding that stayed wet or touched contaminated water are usually replaced. The readings tell you which is which.

Common Questions

Structural drying questions from Saint Peters homeowners

How do I know if my walls are still wet after a leak?

Signs include a musty smell, cool or damp-feeling drywall, peeling paint, baseboards that swell and a humid room. A moisture meter and a thermal camera show wet areas inside the wall without opening it.

How long does structural drying take?

Drying often takes several days, and the equipment stays until readings reach the target. The time depends on the material, the amount of water, the humidity and how quickly drying started.

Why can I not just run a fan and a household dehumidifier?

Household equipment moves far less air and removes far less moisture than commercial air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. It also cannot tell you when the structure is dry. Readings do that.

Will the equipment be loud and raise my electric bill?

Drying equipment is noticeable, and it does add to power use during the job. The crew explains the setup and the expected duration, and it comes out when the readings meet the standard.
