Act Fast

When water extraction is urgent

Water extraction is urgent whenever water is standing on a floor or saturating carpet. Every hour the water sits, it wicks into baseboards, drywall and subfloor. If you are in Saint Peters and water is on the floor, call the 24/7 line at (636) 747-8964 and stop the source if you can.

The dispatcher will ask about the depth, the source and whether power is affected. Same-day response is the goal, and the call is answered at any hour.

  1. Stop the source: close the valve, shut off the appliance or the main.
  2. Keep power off to wet areas unless the panel is reachable from dry ground.
  3. Lift furniture, rugs and boxes off the wet floor.
  4. Photograph the water and the damage before cleanup.
Equipment

What extraction equipment does the work

Submersible pumps

Move deep water from basements and low areas quickly.

Truck-mount extractors

Use powerful vacuum from the truck to pull water from carpet and pad.

Portable extractors and wet vacs

Handle rooms where truck hoses cannot reach, and tight spots.

Weighted extraction tools

Press water out of carpet and upholstery before drying.

Extraction is not just surface work. Water goes into pad, under baseboards and into the lower inches of drywall, and the crew follows it with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera. Removing as much liquid as possible cuts drying time and reduces the chance of mold.

Next Steps

What happens after the water is out

Once the standing water is gone, the crew decides what can be dried in place and what must be removed. Clean water on carpet may be saved, but contaminated water on any porous material usually is not. The decision follows ANSI/IICRC S500 water categories.

Drying then starts with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. The structural drying page explains how readings decide when the equipment leaves. For carpet, see carpet water damage, and for wood floors, hardwood floor water damage.

Cost factors and insurance

Cost depends on the volume of water, the square footage affected, how many hours extraction takes and whether materials must be removed. Scope and price are explained before work starts. Insurance often treats extraction as part of mitigation, which most policies expect you to start promptly.

Avoid

Mistakes to avoid in the first hour

  • Using a household vacuum on standing water, which is an electrical hazard.
  • Mopping contaminated water into more of the floor.
  • Leaving wet rugs and boxes in place, where they wick moisture into the floor.
  • Running heaters at wet carpet, which can trap moisture in the pad.
  • Waiting for the water to dry on its own.

Stop the source, lift what you can and let the equipment do the heavy removal.

Safety

What to do before help arrives

  • Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.
  • Keep power off in wet rooms unless the panel is dry and accessible.
  • Wear boots and gloves if the water may be contaminated.
  • Lift what you safely can off the floor.
  • Do not leave water standing overnight.
Common Questions

Water extraction questions from Saint Peters homeowners

Do you handle water extraction at night or on weekends in Saint Peters?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. Crews are sent as soon as one is available, and extraction is usually the first job on arrival.

Is a shop vac enough for standing water?

For a small spill, yes. For anything more than a few gallons, or water in carpet and pad, a shop vac leaves most of the water behind. Truck-mount or portable extractors and submersible pumps move far more.

What is the difference between extraction and drying?

Extraction removes liquid water. Drying removes the moisture that remains in materials and air, using air movers and dehumidifiers. Both steps are needed.

Can carpet be extracted and saved?

Often, if the water is clean and the carpet is extracted quickly. Pad is frequently removed because it holds water. See the carpet page for the details.
