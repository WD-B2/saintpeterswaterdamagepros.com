When an appliance leak is an emergency

An appliance leak is an emergency when water is running out onto the floor or spreading into a cabinet base or the room next door. Washing machine hoses, dishwasher supply lines and refrigerator ice-maker tubes can release gallons per minute under pressure. In Saint Peters, call the 24/7 line at (636) 747-8964 while you close the valve behind the appliance.

Same-day response is the goal, and the dispatcher stays on the line to help you shut off water and protect the area. Overnight and weekend calls are answered the same way, because appliances fail while people are asleep or away.

  1. Close the supply valves behind the appliance. If they will not turn or are out of reach, close the main water valve.
  2. Unplug the appliance only if the outlet and cord are dry. If water is near the outlet, call the utility or 911 first.
  3. Move rugs, furniture and boxes from the wet floor, and pull the appliance out only if it is safe and the floor is not slick.
  4. Photograph the leak, the hose and the damaged floor before you clean up.
Sources

The appliance failures that cause most losses

Washing machine supply hoses

A burst hose floods a laundry room and can run through the floor to the room below.

Dishwasher supply and drain lines

Leaks soak the cabinet base and the subfloor under the kitchen.

Refrigerator water lines

A slow leak from an ice maker line can go unnoticed under the fridge for weeks.

Water softeners and humidifiers

Drain and supply fittings can weep or burst when left unattended.

The line between a slow leak and a sudden one matters for insurance and for drying. A hose that fails suddenly and floods a room gets one kind of response. A line that has been wetting a cabinet base for months has often led to mold and rotted material, which changes the scope of the repair.

The Work

What the cleanup involves

The crew finds the path the water took with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera, then extracts standing water from the floor and from carpet if present. Vinyl, tile and laminate floors can hold water beneath them, and appliances often sit on that kind of flooring. Cabinet toe-kicks and lower drywall are checked and opened if they are saturated.

Drying equipment is positioned to push air under cabinets and across the subfloor, and the crew logs readings each visit so you can see when the area reaches the dry standard. The kitchen water damage page covers cabinets and the sink area in more detail, and structural drying explains the equipment.

Cost

Cost factors and insurance

Cost depends on how much water escaped, which rooms it reached and what flooring was wet. A leak that was caught quickly on tile is a modest job, while a leak that ran into a finished basement ceiling is not. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Many insurance policies cover sudden appliance leaks that damage the building, though they treat gradual leaks differently. Keep the failed part, photograph the area and ask your insurer what applies to your policy.

Common Questions

Appliance leak questions from Saint Peters homeowners

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Saint Peters?

Yes. Washers and dishwashers often fail while they run overnight, and the line is answered around the clock. Crews are sent as soon as one is available, and the dispatcher walks you through shutting off the supply.

What do I shut off first if the washing machine is flooding?

Close the two supply valves behind the machine. If you cannot reach them, shut the main water valve. Unplug the machine only if the cord and outlet are dry, and call the utility or 911 if water is near the outlet.

Is a refrigerator or dishwasher leak covered by insurance?

Sudden appliance leaks that damage the building are often covered, while slow leaks over months often are not. Check your policy and keep the failed hose or part for the adjuster.

Can water from a leak under a dishwasher or fridge damage the subfloor?

Yes. It can travel under flooring, soak the subfloor and wick into cabinets. Moisture mapping finds the full path, because the wet area is rarely just the visible puddle.
