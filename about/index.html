Overview

What this site is for

Saint Peters Water Damage Pros is a water damage line and information site for homeowners and businesses in Saint Peters, Missouri and the neighboring communities. When a pipe bursts, a basement floods or a ceiling sags, the first need is a person who answers, asks the right questions and gets a crew moving. That is the job of the line at (636) 747-8964.

The rest of the site exists to help you decide what to do before and after you call. The service pages explain how each type of loss is handled, the location pages cover the nearby communities and the guides explain insurance, drying, mold and cost factors in plain language.

Dispatch

How a call works

  1. You call and describe the source of the water, the address and what is wet.
  2. The dispatcher gives you safety and shut-off steps while you stay on the line.
  3. A crew is sent as soon as one is available, and you are told what to expect.
  4. The crew extracts water, maps moisture and explains the scope and price before work starts.
  5. Drying continues until readings meet the dry standard, and you receive logs and photos.

Same-day response is the goal, and we do not promise an arrival time. In a widespread storm or flood, calls are worked in the order they arrive.

Services

What we do

The line covers twenty services, from burst pipes and water heaters to flooding, sewage backup, mold and commercial losses. The full services page lists them by group. Each service page includes a cost-factor section and a note on insurance, so you can see what drives scope before you call.

Standing water is extracted, wet materials that cannot dry are removed and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. Moisture readings are logged at each visit, and equipment stays until the structure reaches the dry standard.

Approach

Why plain scopes matter

Most regret after a water loss comes from vague scopes. A homeowner agrees to work without knowing which rooms are included, how many days the equipment stays or what happens if more damage turns up behind a wall. The answer is a written scope, readings you can read and a crew that explains each decision.

That is the standard this line is built around. Before work starts, you hear what is wet, what will be removed, what will be dried in place and what the price covers. If something changes, you hear why. We would rather lose a job than have you guess.

Common Questions

Questions about how this works

Do you answer the phone at night and on weekends?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, including holidays. Crews are sent as soon as one is available.

Do you hold a firm-level IICRC certification?

We make no certification claims. We follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 for mold as published standards of practice, and we do not state that the firm holds a certificate.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

We explain scope and price before work starts. The scope depends on what moisture readings show, so the dispatcher describes the cost factors on the call and the crew confirms the scope on site.
