Privacy Policy

A short, plain description of what information the site and the phone line handle, and what we do with it.

LEGAL

What this policy covers

This policy describes how Saint Peters Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website, call our line or send us an email. The site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address, a description of the problem and anything else you choose to tell us. We use that information to dispatch help, to provide the service and to review how calls are handled.

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or end the call. Emergency help, if any, is not conditioned on anything beyond the information needed to send a crew.

Email

If you email contact@saintpeterswaterdamagepros.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell your email address.

Website data and third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may display embedded maps from Google Maps. When your browser loads those items, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses and page requests, for security and operations. We do not use those logs to build individual profiles.

How we use and share information

  • To answer calls and emails and to dispatch crews.
  • To provide, price and perform the service you request.
  • To review calls for quality and training.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We share information with crews and service partners only as needed to perform the work you request, and with others when the law requires. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

We keep call records and emails for as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by law. To ask about information we hold, email us. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

This site and line are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the current version is always on this page. Questions can go to contact@saintpeterswaterdamagepros.com or (636) 747-8964. Our address is 7090-7098 Mexico Road, St. Peters, MO 63376.
