When a frozen pipe is an emergency

A frozen pipe becomes an emergency the minute it thaws and leaks. If you can hear water running in a wall or see it dripping from a ceiling in Saint Peters, close the main valve and call the 24/7 line at (636) 747-8964. The dispatcher will tell you what to shut off and what to move while a crew is on the way.

Do not wait for the leak to stop on its own. A split pipe keeps flowing as long as the supply is open, and the longer it runs inside a wall the more insulation, drywall and framing hold moisture. After-hours and weekend calls get the same response, because freeze breaks happen when the weather turns, not during business hours.

Cause

Why freeze damage is different from other pipe failures

Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pressure splits the pipe or pops a fitting, usually along a pipe wall rather than at a joint. The break is often hidden in an exterior wall, an attic, a crawl space or a garage ceiling. The crack can stay plugged by ice, so the home looks undamaged until the temperature rises.

The National Weather Service St. Louis reported a low of minus 6 degrees Fahrenheit at Lambert Airport on December 22 and 23, 2022, and minus 4 on February 16, 2021. Those are the kinds of nights that crack lines in unheated spaces. If you are expecting a hard freeze, let faucets drip on exterior walls, open cabinet doors under sinks, and keep the thermostat at a steady temperature.

Exterior walls

Lines that run in a wall facing north or west lose heat first, especially behind sinks and tubs.

Attics and garage ceilings

Supply lines above the insulation have no help from the heated living space.

Crawl spaces and slab edges

Unheated voids under the floor let lines freeze even when the house feels warm.

Outdoor spigots and hose bibs

A hose left connected traps water that freezes back into the wall behind the spigot.

The Work

What happens once the water is shut off

The crew starts with moisture mapping to find where the thaw water went. Clean supply water is Category 1, but it can still soak insulation and drywall, and freeze breaks often happen in cavities that stay damp for days. Extraction units pull standing water, wet material is cut out where it cannot dry, and air movers and LGR dehumidifiers take over. See structural drying and dehumidification for how the process runs.

A plumber repairs the pipe, and many homeowners schedule the plumber and the restoration crew on the same day. Ask the plumber to leave the damaged section for the adjuster, and photograph the split before it is cut away.

Costs, insurance and prevention

Cost follows the number of wet rooms, the materials involved and the days of drying. A split in a single basement ceiling is a smaller job than a line that ran through a finished wall. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and the dispatcher can walk through the factors on the phone.

Common Questions

Frozen pipe questions from Saint Peters homeowners

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Saint Peters?

Yes. Cold snaps often break pipes overnight and on weekends, and the line is answered around the clock. Crews are sent as soon as one is available, and the dispatcher tells you how to shut off the water and protect the area while you wait.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Only if the pipe has not burst and you can do it without an open flame. Never use a torch. Open the faucet, warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer and call a plumber if it will not clear. If water is already leaking, shut the main valve and skip the thawing.

Why does the damage show up after the pipe thaws?

Ice can plug a split in the pipe, so nothing leaks while it is frozen. When the ice melts, the full pressure of the supply line pushes water through the crack. That is why a pipe that looked fine in the morning can flood a wall by afternoon.

Does insurance cover water damage from a frozen pipe?

Many policies cover sudden freeze damage, but some have conditions about heating the home or draining the system when it is vacant. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph the split pipe before it is repaired.
