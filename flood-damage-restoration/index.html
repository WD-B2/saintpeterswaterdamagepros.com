When flood water is an emergency

Flood water in a home or business is always an emergency. It can carry sewage, chemicals and debris, and it soaks drywall, insulation, flooring and furniture in a matter of hours. If this is happening in Saint Peters, call the 24/7 line at (636) 747-8964 and stay out of the water.

Do not walk through standing water if electrical outlets, cords or appliances are submerged. Shut off power at the main breaker only if you can reach it from dry ground, and call the utility or 911 if you cannot.

  1. Get everyone out of the water and away from electrical hazards.
  2. Shut off the power at the main only if the panel is dry and reachable.
  3. Do not use household appliances, TVs or vacuums while wet.
  4. Photograph the water line on walls and the damaged belongings before moving anything.
  5. Wear boots and gloves if you must enter, and wash well afterward.
Water Category

What makes flood water different

Water that rises from the ground or enters from outside is treated as Category 3, grossly contaminated, under ANSI/IICRC S500. That changes what is salvaged. Drywall, insulation, carpet and padding that have been soaked by flood water are usually removed and discarded rather than dried.

Porous materials

Carpet, pad, insulation and soft drywall absorb contaminants and are generally removed.

Semi-porous materials

Wood framing and subfloor are cleaned and dried, often with antimicrobial treatment.

Non-porous materials

Concrete, metal and tile are cleaned and sanitized.

Contents

Upholstered items and mattresses are usually lost, while hard goods can often be cleaned.

The Work

The flood cleanup sequence

The crew extracts standing water first, using pumps and extraction units. Contaminated materials are then removed and bagged, and the structure is cleaned and treated before drying. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry framing and subfloor, with readings logged until the structure meets the dry standard.

The sequence for the extraction step is covered on the water extraction page, and the drying step on structural drying and dehumidification. If sewage is involved, see sewage backup cleanup.

Common Questions

Flood damage questions from Saint Peters homeowners

Do you handle flood emergencies at night or on weekends in Saint Peters?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. In a widespread event, crews are sent in the order calls arrive and as soon as one is available.

Is flood water considered contaminated?

Floodwater that comes from outside the building is generally treated as Category 3 under ANSI/IICRC S500 because it can carry soil, bacteria and chemicals. Porous materials it has touched are usually removed rather than dried.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage?

Standard homeowners policies typically exclude flood from rising surface water, and flood coverage is a separate policy. Check your policy and call your agent before assuming either way.

How do I know if my home is in a flood zone?

Check the FEMA flood map for your address or ask the City of St. Peters building department. We do not state zone designations for specific addresses.
