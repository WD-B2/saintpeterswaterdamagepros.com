The Call

What happens when you call

The dispatcher asks where you are, where the water came from and what it has touched. You will be asked about power, standing water depth and whether anyone is in danger. Those answers decide the first steps, and they decide what equipment is sent.

You are told what to expect next. We do not promise an arrival time, but the goal is same-day response and the call is answered at any hour. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Before You Call

What to prepare

  • Your street address and a nearby cross street.
  • The source of the water and whether it is stopped.
  • Which rooms or floors are wet.
  • Whether power is on in the wet area.
  • Whether you have insurance information handy.

Other ways to get help

If you smell gas, see sparks or fear a structural collapse, call 911 first. For electrical hazards in standing water, call the utility. Once everyone is safe, call (636) 747-8964 to start the water side of the response.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and S520 covers mold remediation. This page names them as references only, so you can compare any plan you receive against the same yardstick.

Common Questions

Contact questions

What should I have ready when I call?

Your street address, the source of the water if you know it, which rooms are wet, how long it has been going and whether power is on in the wet area. If you have photos, keep them handy.

Is the line really answered at night?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. Crews are sent as soon as one is available.

Can I send photos by email?

Yes. Email contact@saintpeterswaterdamagepros.com with the address and a short description. For anything urgent, call first.

Do you have a contact form?

No. We use the phone and email only.
