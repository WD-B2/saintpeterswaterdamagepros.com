We do not claim that any type of water damage is more common here than anywhere else in St. Peters. Water heaters, supply lines, washing machine hoses and dishwashers wear with use in any house, so these are the leak sources worth checking. First Steps

What to do if water is still spreading

STEP 1

Check for hazards

If the leak is near a water heater or electrical equipment, stay back and do not stand in the water. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility.

STEP 2

Stop the water

Close the cold water supply valve on the tank or appliance, or the main valve if that is easier to reach safely.

STEP 3

Clear the floor

Move small items, shoes and cleaning supplies away from the wet area.

STEP 4

Document it

Take photographs of the leaking equipment, the wet floor and any stains that have spread to nearby walls.

The Process

Extraction, drying and what gets removed

The crew extracts standing water first, then uses a moisture meter to find wet flooring, baseboards and wall material. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the area while readings are logged. Wet carpet pad is often removed, and carpet may or may not be dried in place depending on how long it was wet and how clean the water was.

The S500 standard is the industry reference for water damage work. We name it only as a yardstick so you can compare any plan you receive against the same expectations.

Claims

Documenting the loss

Take photos, note when you discovered the leak and keep any plumber or repair invoice. Ask for moisture readings and a written scope. These records can help describe the damage to your insurer, whose decision depends on your policy and the cause of loss.

Which school district applies?

Sales records for homes here list the St. Charles R-VI School District, but district lines differ by address, so confirm with the district.

What if a water heater has failed?

Shut off the cold water supply to the tank and, for an electric unit, power at the breaker only from dry ground. Call the line, and a gas unit with a smell of gas needs the gas utility first.

Do I need to move furniture before the crew arrives?

Lift small items off wet floors if you can do so safely, but leave heavy furniture. The crew moves it as part of the work.
